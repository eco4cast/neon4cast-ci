Phenology (the changes in plant canopies over the year) has been identified as one of the primary ecological fingerprints of global climate change.

Greenness and redness, as measured by a camera looking down at the top of vegetation, are quantitative measures of phenology. The phenology theme challenges you to forecast daily mean greenness and/or redness at up to 47 terrestrial NEON sites.

Learn more at: <https://projects.ecoforecast.org/neon4cast-docs/Phenology.html>
