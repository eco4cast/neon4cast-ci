The exchange of water and carbon dioxide between the atmosphere and the land is akin to the breathing rate and lung capacity of Earth's terrestrial ecosystems.

The terrestrial flux theme challenges you to forecast the gas exchange at up to 47 sites across the U.S.

**Supporting data.** The 30-minute targets are designed to support models that require sub-daily data. Forecasts from sub-daily models should be aggregated to a daily mean before submitting to the Challenge. The 30-minute data are at:

```
https://sdsc.osn.xsede.org/bio230014-bucket01/challenges/targets/project_id=neon4cast/duration=PT30M/terrestrial_30min-targets.csv.gz
```

Learn more at: <https://projects.ecoforecast.org/neon4cast-docs/Terrestrial.html>
